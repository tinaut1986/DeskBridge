- The desktop can now show one of its screens on the laptop: the laptop's panel becomes one more monitor, placed where it sits on the Layout map.
- Pause screen sharing and Independent machines (on Home and in the tray menu) turn sharing off without changing the configuration; in an emergency, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Home turns on Independent machines on any computer.
- When a computer switches off, restarts or leaves the network, the others notice, stop showing it and pick up again when it is back, also after a network change.
- The Virtual Display Driver is offered on every computer that needs it, restarted by itself if it stops, and given any resolution it is missing.
- Fixes: changing the primary display works again; screen borders keep their colour, stay above the taskbar and only show while another computer is connected; the lock screen service starts again after an update.
- A plain message when the graphics driver stops working, instead of retrying again and again.
