- One keyboard and mouse for several PCs: push the cursor past the edge of a display to move it to the other machine; Ctrl+Alt+Home always brings it back.
- Show a PC's screen on another PC's monitor: the laptop gets a virtual display (Virtual Display Driver) that is streamed with low latency to the monitor you choose.
- Layout tab: place the other machines' displays on your desk at their real size and scale, and choose in profiles what each monitor shows.
- Clipboard and file sharing between the machines (off by default), over an encrypted connection protected by a shared key.
- Works with a firewall you cannot change: the machine behind it connects out to the coordinator.
- Updates itself from these releases.
