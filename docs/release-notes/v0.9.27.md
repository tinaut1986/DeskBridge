- A new look: a side bar with one page for each part of the app.
- Sound: hear the other PC's sound on the coordinator, with its own volume, and use the coordinator's microphone on the other PC (with VB-CABLE).
- Smoother shared displays: less delay, the pointer drawn at once, correct colours, almost no network use while the screen is still, and an option to show the picture without waiting for the screen refresh.
- Lock screen and administrator prompts: an optional service lets you see and use them from the coordinator; "Run as administrator" makes apps opened as administrator take the keyboard and mouse.
- Layout: right-click a display to make it the primary display of its machine; each display can be marked with the colour of its machine.
- Installs as an app (Start menu, Settings > Apps) and can be updated remotely from the coordinator.
